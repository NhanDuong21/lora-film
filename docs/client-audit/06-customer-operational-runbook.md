# Customer operational runbook

1. Login and browse a now-showing movie.
2. Choose cinema/date/showtime, then open seat selection.
3. Select available seats and create a held booking once.
4. Choose concessions and eligible promotion/voucher.
5. Verify ticket, food, discount and final totals before payment.
6. Complete or fail the payment through an enabled provider.
7. Verify payment return, booking result, issued tickets and history/detail.

Refresh/back/forward, expiry, already-held/sold seats, double submit, invalid voucher, payment failure and cross-account detail are mandatory negative scenarios.
